VORRATH

— The Beast of Galaxy —

Born from the shadowed side of Nexus Infinitum's energy — unplanned, unwanted. Vorrath has no noble purpose, no ambition. It has only one instinct — to destroy everything. Once the size of a black hole, now chained and awakened, Vorrath roams free once more.

💀 WEAPON
DOOMCLAW
Not a crafted weapon — Doomclaw is part of Vorrath's own body, formed from hundreds of galaxies he has destroyed. A massive mace that grows larger with every civilization it crushes.
📖 LORE — THE BEAST OF GALAXY
🔙 — ORIGIN —

Before the first galaxy formed, before time knew itself — something moved within the void. Not light. Not darkness. Something older than both.

When the Nexus Infinitum exploded and gave birth to the multiverse, not all of its energy became light. Some of it fell into the cracks — unplanned, unwanted. A part that was never meant to exist.

From there, Vorrath was formed.

⚡ — NATURE OF HIS POWER —

Sovarion controls technology. Sovarion chooses when to fight.

Vorrath controls nothing. Because Vorrath is destruction itself.

Not because he is evil. Not because he hates.

But because that is how he exists — like fire cannot choose not to burn.

💀 — WHY THE AUTHORITY FEARS HIM —

The Authority of Galaxy is not afraid of Vorrath's power.

They fear something far simpler —

No one can stop him.

Not because he is too strong. But because stopping Vorrath is like stopping the entropy of the universe itself.

You cannot negotiate with gravity.

🌌 — HIS ROLE IN EPIC WARS —

For 5000 years, he slept. Chained. Weakened by the purple crystals on a planet whose name almost no one remembered.

Then someone freed him.

Why? No one knows.

What is certain — when Vorrath opened his eyes again and gazed upon the multiverse that had changed during his slumber — Earth had already become the center of everything.

And Vorrath... was drawn to it.
