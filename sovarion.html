SOVARION

— Guardian of the Multiverse —

An ordinary human — yet with intelligence and technology that made even cosmic entities think twice. Sovarion is calm, wise, and fights only when necessary. His advanced technology has earned him the title of a demigod, yet he remains nothing more than a brilliant human being.

⚔️ WEAPON
ASTRAEBLADE
A hunter sword radiating green cosmic energy. Forged from the core of dying stars, Astraeblade is an extension of Sovarion's very soul — capable of cutting through dimensions with a single strike.
⚡ ABILITIES
COSMIC SLASH
A swing of Astraeblade that releases a wave of cosmic energy, piercing through dimensions.
MULTIVERSE SHIELD
Summons a shield from the energy of 7 dimensions, reflecting incoming attacks.
CELESTIAL BURST
Releases a star energy explosion that burns all enemies in a wide area.
⭐ ULTIMATE — GUARDIAN NOVA
Sovarion merges with the Nexus Infinitum — radiating celestial light that destroys all darkness within a galactic radius.
