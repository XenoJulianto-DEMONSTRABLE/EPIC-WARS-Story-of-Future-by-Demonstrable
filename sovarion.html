SOVARION

— Guardian of the Multiverse —

An ordinary human — yet with intelligence and technology that made even cosmic entities think twice. Sovarion is calm, wise, and fights only when necessary. His advanced technology has earned him the title of a demigod, yet he remains nothing more than a brilliant human being.

⚔️ WEAPON
ASTRAEBLADE
A hunter sword radiating green cosmic energy. Forged from the core of dying stars, Astraeblade is an extension of Sovarion's very soul — capable of cutting through dimensions with a single strike.
📖 LORE

Sovarion sat alone at the edge of cosmic darkness. His eyes gazed at the planets slowly spinning before him, starlight reflecting off his visor. Silent. Calm. Yet behind that stillness, his mind drifted far away.

Far into a time he had long left behind.

— FLASHBACK —

A city. Dark. Consumed by corruption and crime. A young child lived abandoned by his family — left behind by an unjust wave of massive riots. The child grew up alone. Only hatred kept him company.

The child chose the dark path. Surviving by any means necessary.

Yet his mind never stopped. Technology spoke to him in ways that could not be explained — the child understood it, built it, created it. And slowly, it changed him. At night the child roamed in his suit — taking no side, fighting all that was wrong. By day the child was known as a successful man that no one ever suspected.

Until one day, the young man looked up at the sky and decided — the young man wanted to know what lay beyond. With a rocket built by his own hands, the young man launched into space. And out there, the young man witnessed three great factions at war. The young man saw the same injustice he had always known. And the young man chose to join The Authority of Galaxy — not because he was ordered to. But because he had always stood for what was right.

— BACK TO THE PRESENT —

Sovarion opened his eyes.

The flashback faded. Yet its weight still lingered with every step.

He gripped Astraeblade tighter.

And then —

A roar tore through the cosmic silence. Not a human voice. Not the sound of any creature that had ever existed in any galaxy.

VORRATH.

Sovarion rose slowly. No fear. No hesitation. Only one thing on his mind —

"This war is not over."

And as the figure of destruction drew near — the epic battle began.
