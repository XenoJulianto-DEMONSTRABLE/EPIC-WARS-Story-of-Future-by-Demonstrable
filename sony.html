📖 LORE

BlackThorn City — a city built on a foundation of lies. Its government is corrupt. Its police are bought. Its people are divided. In this city, massive protests erupt almost every night — yet nothing ever changes. Justice is nothing more than an empty word spoken by those who have never experienced hunger.

Amidst the chaos, a young boy lost everything. His family was left behind — not because they chose to leave, but because the system stripped away their rights one by one. His parents joined a demonstration that ended in a violent crackdown. They never came back.

The boy grew up alone on the streets of BlackThorn. He learned to survive — not through kindness, but through intelligence and crime. His first theft at age 10. Joining a street gang at 13. Becoming one of the masterminds behind BlackThorn's biggest illegal operations by 16.

But there was something different about him. Beneath the sharpness and brutality, there was a fire that never died — an anger that didn't just want to destroy, but wanted to change.

One night, after a mission that nearly cost him his life, he discovered something in the ruins of an old lab outside the city — technology that shouldn't exist. Technology that didn't come from BlackThorn. Technology that didn't even come from Earth.

That was the turning point. He was no longer just a thief. He became something greater.

With an intelligence already far above average, he studied the alien technology in a matter of months. He built a combat suit — black, silent, and lethal. He built a rocket from scrap parts and stolen components. And he did something no one in BlackThorn believed was possible.

He flew into space.

No one knew where he went. But when he returned years later, he was no longer a street kid from BlackThorn. He was something else entirely.

By day, he emerged as a new figure — wealthy, influential, and seemingly unconnected to anything dark. A Bruce Wayne vibe meeting Tony Stark, but colder. More distant. Harder to read.

By night... he became The Black Hyena. A shadow moving between the corridors of power. Taking neither the side of The Authority of Galaxy, nor Queen Nathasias, nor anyone else.

He only stood for one thing: the truth that all sides have buried.

⚔️ ABILITIES

👁️ SHADOW VEIL
Passive — Stealth

His suit absorbs surrounding light, making him virtually invisible in darkness. Even the most advanced sensors struggle to detect his presence when this ability is active.

🌐 HACK REALITY
Active — Technology Manipulation

Using a hidden neural antenna inside his helmet, he can infiltrate any system — from simple security networks to alien military mainframes — in a matter of seconds.

🦷 HYENA'S BITE
Active — Combat

A swift and lethal strike using concealed blades in the suit's forearms. Not as powerful as the Astraeblade, but far faster. Designed to kill before the enemy realizes what happened.

🪐 ZERO GRAVITY SHIFT
Active — Mobility

A miniature gravity module in his suit allows him to move unbound by gravity for up to 8 seconds. Used for impossible maneuvers — running on walls, hovering in mid-air, or escaping any situation.

🛰️ INTEL NETWORK
Passive — Information

An underground information network he built over years. He knows almost everything about every faction — secrets that even The Authority of Galaxy itself is unaware of.

🔄 DUAL IDENTITY
Ultimate — Transformation

Instantly switches between "Day Mode" (no suit, high social presence) and "Night Mode" (full Black Hyena suit, all abilities active). Enemies will never know who they're truly facing.

🪓 WEAPON

🪓
NIGHTFANG BLADES
DUAL INTEGRATED BLADES — STEALTH CLASS

A pair of concealed blades integrated directly into the suit's forearms. Forged from an alloy not registered in any database — most likely a material acquired in space. Lightweight, sharp down to the molecular level, and reflects no light. Unlike the Astraeblade, which stands as a symbol of courage, the Nightfang Blades were designed for one purpose: to kill without being known.
